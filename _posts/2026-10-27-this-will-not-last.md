title: This will not last
---

Nothing on this blog stays whole. From the moment a piece is written, its letters begin to go, a few at first and then more for every day that nobody reads it to the end.

Reading brings them back. Stay with a passage and the missing letters return. Finish a piece and all of it comes back, and then, from that moment, it begins to forget again.

Delete this post when the real writing begins.

## How a post gets here

Make a file in the `_posts` folder named like `2026-11-02-a-title.md`. Put a title between two lines of dashes at the top, then write. That is all.

> Anything worth keeping has to be kept.
