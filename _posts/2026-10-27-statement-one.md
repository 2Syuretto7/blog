This blog is a single outline. Every piece is a numbered statement, and every paragraph inside it is a statement under that one: 1a, 1b, 1c.

You choose how much of it to see. At level one there are only titles. At level two, the first line of everything. At level three, all of it.

The idea is borrowed from a system shown in San Francisco in December 1968, where a man with a wooden mouse folded and unfolded his thoughts on a screen, and the room watched the future arrive early.

Delete this statement when the real writing begins.

> A thought is easier to hold when you can see its shape.
