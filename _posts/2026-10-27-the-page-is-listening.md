This is the first page of a blog that does not exist yet. Everything written here will leave a mark on the other side of the paper, backwards, faint, and visible only to whoever moves slowly enough to look.

Delete this post when the real writing begins.

## How a post gets here

Make a file in the `_posts` folder named like `2026-11-02-a-title.md`. Put a title between two lines of dashes at the top, then write. That is all.

> A sentence is a small machine for changing a mind. Build one that runs.
