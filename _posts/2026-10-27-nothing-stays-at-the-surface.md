title: Nothing stays at the surface
---

Everything written here starts just below the waterline and sinks eighteen metres a day. In a week a post has left the sunlight. In two months it is past a kilometre, where no sunlight reaches. In a year and a half it will reach the floor of the Challenger Deep, eleven kilometres down.

To read something old, you have to go down to where it is. The deeper the writing, the longer the fall, the heavier the pressure, and the less light there is to read it by. Down there, anything you touch gives off a little light of its own.

Delete this post when the real writing begins.

## How a post gets here

Make a file in the `_posts` folder named like `2026-11-02-a-title.md`. Put a title between two lines of dashes at the top, then write. That is all.

> Whatever you let go of keeps going down.
