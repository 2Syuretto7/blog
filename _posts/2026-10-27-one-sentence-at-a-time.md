---
title: One sentence at a time
---

The front page of this blog never shows a whole piece of writing. It shows one sentence, pulled loose from wherever it was written, and nothing else.

Every sentence has to be able to stand up on its own out there. Click and another one arrives from somewhere else entirely. Follow the title underneath and you land inside the piece it came from, at the exact place it was taken.

Delete this post when the real writing begins.

> Write every sentence as if it might be the only one anyone reads.
